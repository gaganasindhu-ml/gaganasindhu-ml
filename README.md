## 🏗️ What I'm Building

**[ClearDraw](https://cleardraw.vercel.app)** — a B2B SaaS that automates subcontractor compliance tracking and draw management for US general contractors.

- **Live, end-to-end product**: auth, document upload + preview, approve/reject workflows, pending-review tracking, and 90-day expiring upload links
